Fantasy HUD Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

Contact: fantasyhudsupport@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Fantasy HUD is a companion application for Amazon Fire TV that displays live fantasy football scores, matchups, and roster information from ESPN Fantasy Football and Sleeper.

This policy covers the Fantasy HUD Fire TV application. The separate iPhone companion application may have different data practices.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the services you connect, Fantasy HUD processes:

  • Sleeper usernames and user identifiers
  • ESPN and Sleeper league identifiers
  • Fantasy team names and team identifiers
  • Fantasy roster, player, matchup, and scoring information
  • Locally saved application preferences
  • Your Fire TV's local IP address for phone pairing

Fantasy HUD does not require users to create a separate Fantasy HUD account.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to retrieve and display fantasy football information, maintain selected accounts and teams, refresh scores, operate the Fire TV overlay, and support local pairing with a phone.

Fantasy HUD may refresh connected fantasy information approximately every 30 seconds while active.

4. ESPN and Sleeper Connections

Fantasy HUD communicates directly with ESPN and Sleeper services to retrieve the fantasy football information requested by users.

These services receive relevant network requests and identifiers necessary to provide the selected features. Their own privacy policies govern information processed by those services.

Fantasy HUD is an independent application and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by ESPN or Sleeper.

5. Local Data Storage

Fantasy HUD stores selected fantasy account preferences, league selections, roster information, and display settings locally on the Fire TV device.

The current release configuration disables Android application backups and excludes application preferences from operating-system backup and device-transfer rules.

Users can remove locally stored information by clearing Fantasy HUD's application data in Fire TV settings or uninstalling the application.

6. Advertising and Analytics

Fantasy HUD does not display advertisements.

The reviewed Fire TV application does not include developer-operated advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

This statement does not cover independent data practices of ESPN, Sleeper, the operating system, or the separate iPhone companion application.

7. Phone Pairing

Fantasy HUD can display a QR code containing the Fire TV's local IP address. This allows the companion iPhone application to connect over the user's local network and control supported display features.

8. Children's Privacy

The initial Fantasy HUD Amazon Appstore listing is intended for users aged 18 and older.

9. Data Deletion and User Choices

Users may change their selected fantasy account or league in the application.

Locally stored Fantasy HUD settings and information can be deleted by clearing application data or uninstalling Fantasy HUD from the Fire TV device.

Questions or requests relating to Fantasy HUD data can be sent to fantasyhudsupport@gmail.com.

Information maintained by ESPN or Sleeper must be managed through those respective services.

10. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as Fantasy HUD features and information practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
